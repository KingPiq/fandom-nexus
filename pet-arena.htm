❓ How to play

📱 Mobile

🕹️ Hold the joystick or ▲◀▼▶ buttons to walk.
🔎 Interact enters caves, uses portals and starts a battle with a monster next to you.
🏃 Run toggles sprinting (uses stamina).
💚 Rest heals your pet when you are not in a battle.
👆 You can also tap a monster or a cave door on the map.

⌨️ Keyboard

WASD or arrow keys: move · Shift: run · E or Enter: interact · Esc: leave cave or close help · H: this help · F: fullscreen.

⚔️ Battles

Attack deals damage. ✨ Special needs to be bought in the shop and has a 3 turn cooldown. 💚 Heal costs 5 coins. 🏃 Escape costs 50 coins and only works 35% of the time. The maximum level is 25.

⚔️ Monster difficulty

Regular mobs have 15% more health and attack and appear about twice as often. Boss stats stay unchanged, and boss lairs become available again after 3 victories instead of 5. Only admins can skip the in-game time.

⚡ Stamina & speed

👑 Bosses & avatar

Every region has a boss lair (its X/Y is shown under the region name). Regular monsters spawn at varied levels up to level 25; every boss is level 25. Bosses cannot be escaped, use a heavy strike every 3rd turn, and drop 3 rare shards. Press 👕 Avatar to style your trainer; premium items cost 💠 diamonds.

💬 Chat & treasure

Press / to chat (type /help for commands). Bad words and personal info are hidden as ####. 🐾 Your equipped pet follows you around the map. 📦 Walk over treasure chests for coins, shards or a heal. ✨ Shiny monsters give double rewards. 💥 Attacks crit 15% of the time, and win streaks give bonus coins.

🛒 Shop

Win battles for coins, then press 🛒 Buy on a pet or buy the Special attack. Regions unlock at 3 and 8 wins.
